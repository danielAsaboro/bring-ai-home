Motion alerts are noisy, rules are brittle, and the event you care about disappears inside hours of footage.

**QVAC capabilities:** Multimodal · Image classification · Text generation

## Before and after

| | |
|---|---|
| **Without AI** | Arm sources and configure time and motion rules by hand. |
| **With QVAC** | Describe a condition in plain words, inspect the proposed watcher, replay recorded events, and tune out false positives. |

## What you will build
