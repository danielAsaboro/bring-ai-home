Written procedures are expensive to create, go stale, and make people switch constantly between the guide and the task.

**QVAC capabilities:** Transcription · Multimodal · Text generation · Embeddings

## Before and after

| | |
|---|---|
| **Without AI** | Write and run a five-step weekly-report tape by hand. |
| **With QVAC** | Narrate and demonstrate once. Replay proposes steps, parameters, preconditions, and stop conditions, then guides a dry run. |
