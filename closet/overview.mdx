People struggle to see their wardrobe as combinations, remember what works, and pack without overpacking.

**QVAC capabilities:** Multimodal · Image classification · Embeddings · Text generation

## Before and after

| | |
|---|---|
| **Without AI** | Catalogue garments, assemble a look, and pack a trip by hand. |
| **With QVAC** | Add a garment photo, review the proposed details, then ask for looks from clean items for an occasion and a packing limit. |
