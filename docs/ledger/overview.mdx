Typing transactions by hand is tedious, but sending statements and receipts to a remote parser hands over your whole financial history.

**QVAC capabilities:** OCR · Text generation · Embeddings

## Before and after

| | |
|---|---|
| **Without AI** | Import a CSV and categorize entries by hand. |
| **With QVAC** | Read a statement or receipt locally, review every candidate with its source and confidence, and watch the totals stay exact, because arithmetic never touches the model. |
