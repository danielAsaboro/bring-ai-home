**Bring AI Home** is a free, hands-on school for local AI. You do not build chat demos here. You take a useful app that already works, and you add private, on-device intelligence with [QVAC](https://qvac.tether.io), one lesson at a time. Each lesson comes with a video, a written walkthrough, a tested checkpoint, and a receipt that proves it worked.
