---
title: "Why bring AI home?"
description: "Local intelligence belongs close to the private context that makes an application useful."
---

Documents, spending histories, family photos, relationship notes, and home records contain context people may hesitate to upload to a remote service.

Bring AI Home starts with applications that already help organize that context. The course explores adding local OCR, transcription, generation, and retrieval through QVAC.

## Start with a working product

A model should improve an existing workflow. Document review, accounting arithmetic, editing, and record keeping still need to work independently of model availability.

## Keep proposals reviewable

Extracted text can be wrong. A suggested transaction, document mark, or memory needs a review step before it becomes an accepted record.

## Keep responsibilities explicit

Models propose interpretations. Application code handles deterministic calculations, state changes, and audit history. People make the final decisions.
