# Lumen Kettle strategy memo (fictional, internal)

## Retail position
Lumen sits between the two best-selling kettles on shelf price. Partner buyers told us they lack a mid-priced model with a visible feature to demo.

## Launch window
We plan a twelve-week pilot in 40 partner stores starting in March. Store staff get a ten-minute demo script in week one.

## Margin plan
Target retail margin is 34 percent. Replacement lids and bases are sold separately and keep the kettle in use longer.
