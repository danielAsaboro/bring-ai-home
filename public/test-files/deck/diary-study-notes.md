# Kitchen diary study notes (fictional)

## Who took part
Twelve households kept a two-week morning diary. Eight had children at home.

## Unattended boiling
In 9 of 12 homes the kettle boiled while nobody was in the kitchen. People reboiled water an average of 1.6 times per morning.

## Temperature guessing
Tea drinkers guessed the water temperature by sound. Most said they waited "until it stops rumbling".
