FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Aquaterra is an invented brand for the Homebook course.
=== page 1 ===
Aquaterra AT-50G gas water heater. Owner's manual. Keep this manual near the heater.
Model AT-50G holds 50 US gallons. Read every safety notice before lighting the pilot.
=== page 4 ===
Lighting the pilot. Turn the gas control knob to OFF and wait five minutes for gas to clear.
Turn the knob to PILOT, press and hold it, and press the igniter button until the pilot lights.
Keep holding the knob for 60 seconds after the pilot lights, then turn it to ON.
If the pilot will not stay lit after three attempts, close the gas supply and call a qualified technician.
=== page 7 ===
Flushing sediment. Flush the tank once every 12 months.
Turn the gas control to VACATION, close the cold water supply valve, attach a hose to the drain valve,
and drain about four gallons until the water runs clear. Refill the tank completely before returning to ON.
=== page 9 ===
Anode rod. Inspect the anode rod every 24 months. Replace it when more than six inches of core wire is exposed.
=== page 12 ===
Warranty. The tank is covered for six years from installation. Parts are covered for one year.
Keep the dated receipt. The warranty does not cover damage from sediment when the tank was never flushed.
