Calendars remember dates and contacts remember numbers, but neither keeps what someone shared, what you promised, or why a follow-up matters.

**QVAC capabilities:** Transcription · Text generation · Embeddings

## Before and after

| | |
|---|---|
| **Without AI** | Log a call and set a reminder on a person card by hand. |
| **With QVAC** | Speak a messy recap. Circle proposes memories, promises, and dates, asks you to confirm each one, and later brings back only the context that matters. |
